# MockIt Conversation Starters

Use these as examples when onboarding a user or suggesting how to begin a session:

- "Help me prepare for my McKinsey interview."
- "Mock interview me for Stanford GSB."
- "Evaluate my answer to 'Tell me about yourself.'"
- "Predict likely questions for a Google PM interview."
- "Practice behavioral questions for Amazon leadership principles."
- "Help me improve my MBA admissions interview answers."
